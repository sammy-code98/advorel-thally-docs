Developer Experience describes how easy, clear, and satisfying it is for a developer to reach a useful outcome. It covers the full journey, including discovery, setup, documentation, tooling, errors, support, and feedback.

Use [Tooling and DX Audits](/tooling-dx-audits) to find friction, improve the learning path with [Documentation Best Practices](/documentation-best-practices), and close the loop through [Feedback Collection](/feedback-collection).
