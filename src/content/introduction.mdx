The DX Guide is a community-built resource for people learning, practicing, or leading Developer Relations. Use it to understand the field, apply practical frameworks, and learn from real community work.

## What this guide is

The guide brings together practical frameworks, real examples, and lessons from Developer Relations and Developer Experience. The DXMentorship community maintains it for the global developer ecosystem.

It exists to:

- explain Developer Relations to people entering the field;
- give practitioners useful strategies, templates, and tools;
- help teams understand how DevRel supports developers and products; and
- document the work and people behind Developer Experience.

## Why we built this

DevRel spans advocacy, education, product feedback, community building, content, and developer experience. Useful knowledge often sits across talks, community conversations, company playbooks, and individual experience.

This guide makes that knowledge easier to find, review, improve, and reuse. Contributors can share what they have learned, build in public, and keep the material current as the field changes.

## Who should use this

This guide is for:

- aspiring and early-career DevRel professionals;
- experienced practitioners who want to mentor or contribute;
- Developer Experience practitioners;
- technical writers, educators, and content creators;
- community managers and ecosystem leads; and
- product teams and founders building for developers.

## A living resource

The guide improves through contributions, technical review, corrections, and firsthand examples. Start with the chapter that matches your current problem, then use the related links to continue learning or contribute what you know.
