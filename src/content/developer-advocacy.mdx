Developer advocacy helps developers succeed with a product while carrying their needs back to the teams that build it. The work combines education, technical communication, community participation, and product feedback.

Explore how advocates teach through [Public Speaking](/public-speaking) and [Content Creation](/content-creation), then learn how trusted practitioners can become [Developer Champions](/developer-champions).
