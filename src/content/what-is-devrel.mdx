---
title: "What is Developer Relations?"
description: "Understand how Developer Relations connects developer success, community insight, and product outcomes."
icon: "lightbulb"
---

Developer Relations, often shortened to DevRel, builds productive relationships between organizations and the developers who use or could use their technology. It helps developers succeed while helping product teams understand developer needs.

Continue with [Definitions and Origins](/definitions-and-origins), review the [Core Pillars](/core-pillars), and learn [Why DevRel Matters](/why-devrel-matters).
